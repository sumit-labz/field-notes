For a long time I was doing the gesture poses for 30 seconds, two minutes, but doing the bean made me realize that if I could reverse the bean exercise, I would draw the bean and then think of human posture. I think that will help me in imagining human figures in various forms. It's a mind-bending exercise. I think I wanna do that for some time, you know, when I do the bean, because one thing I'm really weak at is drawing from imagination. And I think bean, visualizing the bean and then think of the human figure, that gives a lot of clarity in drawing the full figure. So let's try that in the next few days and see how it goes.
