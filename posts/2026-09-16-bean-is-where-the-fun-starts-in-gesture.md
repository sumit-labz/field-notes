For gesture drawing, I'm happy to say I have moved up to drawing the bean, the simple bean shape for the torso. For a long time I was doing the gesture poses for 30 seconds, two minutes, but doing the bean made me realize that if I could reverse the bean exercise, I would draw the bean and then think of human posture.

I think that will help me in imagining human figures in various forms. It's a mind-bending exercise. I want to do that for some time, because one thing I'm really weak at is drawing from imagination. Visualizing the bean and then thinking of the human figure gives a lot of clarity in drawing the full figure. So let's try that in the next few days and see how it goes.
