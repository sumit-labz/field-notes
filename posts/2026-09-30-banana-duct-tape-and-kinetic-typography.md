The morning I woke up, I was frustrated. My work felt mechanical, commercial, devoid of feeling or art. I draw from art movements, but it lacks expression. Art, to me, is feeling expressed—like a note conveying my emotion, or words flowing unfiltered. Anything that's a genuine feeling is art. Yet, I depend on commercial work to live. Then, suddenly, I thought of a banana with duct tape. If that's art, why not kinetic typography? Imagine projecting typography on a mountain, a building. Sell the instructions for it—isn't that art? Once you think conceptually, perspectives shift. Painting on canvas and drawing can be limiting. That's where anti-art, conceptual art comes in.
