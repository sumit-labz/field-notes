I keep starting these panels with an ochre ground because every book on
the subject treats it as settled fact — warm underlayer, cool overlayer,
done. Two days ago that fact stopped holding up.

{{fragment:2026-08-18-142307}}

The ground looked right off the palette and wrong on the wall. Warm to the
point of muddy, and no amount of glazing over it fixed the temperature. I
almost wrote the whole approach off as one of the myths this journey is
supposed to be testing.

{{fragment:2026-08-19-101122}}

Cutting the yellow in half was the whole fix. Not a different pigment, not
a different method — just less of the thing everyone says you need more
of. The myth wasn't the ochre ground itself, it was the confidence about
the ratio.
