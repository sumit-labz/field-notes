I struggle with being playful with the things I'm building — every day, opening the computer and spending a lot of time planning. While I haven't recovered from this pattern, I decided to paint a few words every morning to prioritize playfulness. Looking back, it was helpful, but I couldn't turn it into an atomic habit — like 21 days. I did it for a few days but couldn't take it further. The good thing is my paint brushes finally came out of the cupboard. Does this activity help me break the pattern? I'm 50-50.
