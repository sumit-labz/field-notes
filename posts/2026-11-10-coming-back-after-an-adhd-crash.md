Recovered from a major ADHD crash. I'm coming back after several months to record what actually happened.

As I worked over and over, I continued building, building, thinking it's progress, but I think I was acting out of my fixation habits. I was fixated on certain things, building through AI, reading it. I was repeating my behaviors of hyperfixation while emotionally I was stuck. I was not expressing to anyone, and I was clocking 14-15 hours of work every day, then gym, sleeping hardly. I think my whole brain functioning crashed, and I couldn't figure out how to recover for a long time.

Then I started taking rest and decided to be off work. The most difficult emotion I had to deal with was that nothing is predictable for my ADHD life. I wanted to build software for stories and cinema. I was working on it. But when the crash happened, I realized my brain is not designed to work on one thing for a long period of time. I have to switch.

It took me a long time to convince myself, and I felt very depressed about it: first of all, how to achieve a stable income in today's world, and when it doesn't work, what options are left. It took me a lot of mental power to get back again.

I decided to stabilize my cinema and art practice before I get back to the story building software I was doing. I have mixed emotions now, trying to become more playful in my cinema art. I learned a lot of music recently, creating ostinato and learning so much about sound design. I kept learning and learning while I was on this vacation mode for some time.

But now I want to get back again with good energy, showing up daily. I think that's the biggest pause from this field notes website.
