A few months back, I wrote this quote and stuck it on my monitor: giving yourself support. When I reflected on it for a while, I realized the best thing I could do is invest in myself, in the things and the support I need, because nobody is coming to tell me this. You have to invent tools for yourself that will give you productivity.

As I revamped the design of this website, I realized this is the best thing I have done. Not only this website, I also built a desktop application for myself for tracking weekly goals, tracking time blindness, which happens to most people with ADHD. Making stuff beautiful for yourself, so that you start enjoying it. And the beauty of this website is how I capture things: I take audio notes, then transcribe them and put them out there. It gives a lot of confidence, and I don't know how you get confidence when you are not going to an office, when you don't have people around. All you get is you post stuff, you see it yourself and then you like it yourself. When you like it, you feel more, it gives you self-worth. And I think for ADHD people who have gone through a lot of shame throughout their life, building self-worth by investing in yourself is an immensely profitable, great idea.

As I keep investing in myself on these tools, my blogs, sites, and the applications that I have to manage my time, productivity, systems that I have built, I want to take it to another level now. The next level would be how I work. For the past one month, I'm glued to the computer, working at the desk all the time. This has its advantages and its disadvantages. AI can do a lot of heavy lifting for you, building things really fast, where you are acting like a director. But since I'm coming very close to building the scaffolding for the Studio Zero that I've been planning, I'm realizing that I need to create more workflows in which I get back to the computer with images, my text, and then process them further.

I want to work a lot outside the computer and AI. I can talk to AI with ease, but I want to do more and more with pen and paper, handmade creativity, using my own mind, because with AI you tend to depend on it. I want to improve this relationship. After this, I'm going to think about how I take the system in which I work to the next level: doing things by hand and bringing them to the computer, making it more fun and sitting less on a chair.
