Okay, I am feeling a sense of grief after getting into hyperfixation loops. So the grief comes from inability to connect with people. Times I have started feeling that while I am continuously working, dreaming about big outcomes, working towards them, sometimes failing, sometimes successful, I am sensing the structure which led to hyperfixation has stopped allowing people to get into my life also. So I was a very well connected person before I went through these hyperfixation loops in maybe last one month and I used to talk with people and now I don't connect with people much and I used to give reasons that the need for constant connection is not worth it. But I can feel it now to my bones that due to hyperfixation, I have started distancing from people and it feels awkward now. On one side I started feeling that yes, I have something to give, dedicate my life to big dreams but on the other hand I am feeling the pressure of distance and relationships. So yesterday I was doing EFT therapy, you know, that most of the pressure to perform is actually coming from gender expectation also from the society that I have been always living with because inability to earn money and it could lead to so much frustration within myself. I mean I have so much of expectation from myself. So yesterday I was doing an EFT therapy to train my nervous system that the pressure is real but it doesn't mean that you'll stop living in a fun way. So I think somewhere deep inside two things are coming together, the insecurities of life when you don't earn money and how will you safeguard your future. And the second thing is the hyperfixation disconnecting from people. So both the things are operating and I am aware of the fact that most of the people who have ADHD they end up losing their job and it's very difficult to go back to a job and earn money. I was hesitating to say all this because I thought this will not look good in a public blog but this is also a part, a phase of me that I am currently going through and which may not be very important. I am not sure about that but this is how I felt and that's most important. I mean and I think being an artist the expression is most important whether it comes through writing or art whatever. Saying things out is most important and I can feel the grief till my bones of how not battling the ADHD itself has led to so many problems and I am still not done with it. Now last time I wrote about hyperfixation and what I am gonna do about it and I think I am just beginning to deal with it. There is a deep desire to be so involved in my work that I forget everything but at the same time it leads to disconnection and feeling of loneliness creeps in.

You know, it's a synchronicity after seeing and, you know, immediately in the book I read about to become whole, to become truly individual, you don't become by being alone. And that's so true. And I think the metaphor of women drinking from a broken glass, which appeared as a dream, is so true for people with ADHD that you are getting nourished, but the glass is broken, and that's what the truth is. And people around you should be aware of that, that this is what happens to you. And I think if you say that, it becomes a lot more easier. So, most of the grief that comes from being a person with ADHD, it requires a lot of communication.

As I continue to speak about the grief of ADHD, I am realizing it has become so important to manage the hyperfixation, be in control of everything, and the advice of getting out of hyperfixation and allow imperfection to creep in, to not to be perfect all the time, and living with the insecurity. I think those are the parts to be navigated well, because hyperfixation will lead you to insecurity, and it is needed also, because to produce good work, you need that attention.
