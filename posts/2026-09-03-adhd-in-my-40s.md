In the morning, while I was directing this website, I felt something completely missing: one of my obsessions is myself being human — where I'm able to express myself, who I am as a person, how I feel, what it feels like living with ADHD, the pursuit of so many things but reaching nowhere, self-doubt, dealing with shame, therapy — the things I'm experiencing as being human, and how the pursuit of art and music helps me discover the depth of life. Sometimes it feels like becoming someone who keeps creating stuff and has lost touch with the world — and what happens to that person when he works so hyperfixated, then comes back to the world having lost the emotional connection with everyone around him. I think this is the most difficult aspect of ADHD: you want to feel connected with people, but you're not able to.
