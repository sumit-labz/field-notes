Film, cinema, monologues, music, and sound design — the moving image and
what plays under it. Structure and pacing borrowed from other mediums and
tested against a camera or a mix. The territory with the fewest fragments
and the longest gaps between them — it tends to arrive in bursts rather
than a steady practice, and the archive should be honest about that shape.
