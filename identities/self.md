Being human, plainly — who I am underneath the other territories, not what
I make. ADHD, self-doubt, shame, therapy, the pursuit of art and music as a
way to find the depth of things, and what happens to the people around me
when I disappear into hyperfixation and come back having lost the thread
with them. The territory with the least craft and the most exposure.
