Opening note: what made me curious about this. Watching *The Killer* pulled
me toward writing monologues — a character who sounds true despite what he
does. Satire feels like the honest register for that right now, more than
anything straightforwardly serious. Working through John Truby's *The
Anatomy of Story* alongside it to see if I can actually build one.

How it changed: it started as monologues — one voice, one character talking
himself into being true. Somewhere in Truby the monologue stopped being
enough. A voice needs a world to push against, other people to want things
from. So the box was relabelled: same desk, same notebook, but it's story
writing now.
