Opening note: what made me curious about this. Building this archive out
loud made it obvious that one obsession was missing entirely — myself,
being human. Not the work, not the craft, just what it actually feels like
to live with ADHD: the pursuit of so many things and reaching nowhere, the
self-doubt, the shame, the therapy, and the strange cost of hyperfixation —
disappearing into making something, then surfacing to find the emotional
thread with the people around me has gone slack.
