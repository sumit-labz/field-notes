Opening note: what made me curious about this. I realized unless I master composition, my dream of making art will stay stalled — no amount of drawing skill alone gets me there. Starting small and fun: open a landscape on the monitor and squint it down to a 3-value scale. No finished pieces required, just building the eye for structure before color ever enters the picture.
