Opening note: what made me curious about this. Every time I open a painting
book the myths get more airtime than the paint handling — Ochre came from
this cave, ultramarine cost more than gold, the ground has to be prepared
just so. I want to find out which of those stories actually change how I
paint and which ones are just decoration I've been repeating.
